# Night Chapters — Vision

**Tagline:** *I want to see. I play.*  
**Working feel:** Gentle night flight · wonder-first · personal pins · observatory glass (Aladin spine when implemented)

## Soul

You’re the pilot of a **personal night**.  
The chapter is a flight plan written in **memory and mystery**, not ICAO codes.

- **See:** real or real-feeling sky; mystery without scrubbing soul; your labels on the glass.  
- **Play:** steer, choose path, name, rest, postcard — verbs are playful, not operational.  
- **Not Dragon Eye:** no tiers, no conjunction-as-alarm, no military chrome that *means* military.

## Tone

- Bedtime glass cockpit  
- Quiet wonder; optional companion voice (non-blocking)  
- Soft rainy hold allowed  
- No crash cam, no “mission failed”  
- Replay: same sky, new emotion; seasonal nights; house-authored chapters later  

## Success

You finish a Night and feel *flown*, not *drilled*.  
You named a mystery only your house understands.  
You’d fly again when spoons are low — glide is rest, not grind.

## Product fit

Natural extension of Dragon Lady’s Observatory (free-glide + pins + P-to-pin).  
Night Chapters can live as a mode/chapter layer over the same glass — this repo holds the game/chapter identity and schema.

## Guardrails

- Wonder-first  
- Easy pin create *and* delete  
- Public name soft; intimate layers private  
- Human gate on public share  
- If a mechanic only makes sense on a battlespace globe — cut or rename until it only means wonder
